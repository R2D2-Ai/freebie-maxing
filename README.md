# Freebie Maxing

**Mission:** *Freebie Maxing is an open-source project dedicated to helping the everyday person discover, understand, and maximize the free resources, tools, and benefits available on the web. We believe that taking advantage of opportunities shouldn't be gatekept behind complex jargon or endless searching. Our goal is to build a fun, accessible, and community-driven platform that puts the power of "freebie maxing" into the hands of the common man.*

## Roadmap
* **Phase 1 (Live):** Client-side Metro Vancouver prototype with zero user-tracking (`index.html`).
* **Phase 2 (In Progress):** Building the `/scripts/ai_parser.js` pipeline to automate the extraction of corporate T&Cs into strict JSON schemas using LLMs.
